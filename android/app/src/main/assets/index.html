Help & Glossary

Quick guide and reference for xChat offline messaging.

What is xChat?

A serverless, peer-to-peer encrypted messenger designed for situations where normal internet or cellular service is unavailable. Messages travel directly between devices over Wi-Fi, local networks, BLE mesh, or ad-hoc connections.

How do I connect to a peer?

Share your @username, invite link, or IP:port address (shown on Chats tab). Your peer enters it under "New chat" or scans your QR code. Both devices must be on the same local network or in BLE range.

What does "End-to-End Encrypted" mean?

Only you and the recipient can read messages. Each message is encrypted with ChaCha20-Poly1305 using keys negotiated via X3DH and rotated with the Double Ratchet. No intermediaries can decrypt them.

Safety Verification (Emoji Blocks)

Each connection derives a deterministic sequence of 6 emoji symbols from peer identity keys. Compare them in person to mathematically verify that no third party is intercepting your chat.

Anonymous routing (Mixnet)

Wraps messages in Sphinx onion encryption and introduces random delay jitter (20–120ms) and cover traffic to obscure communication patterns from network eavesdroppers.

Disappearing rooms

Ephemeral group channels where message history lives solely in device RAM and is immediately purged upon leaving.

Emergency SOS

Broadcasts an encrypted distress beacon to all reachable peers. Requires holding the button for 2 full seconds to prevent accidental triggers.

Transport status labels

Working: Live, functioning hardware transport (TCP socket over Wi-Fi/LAN, BLE mesh).
Simulated: Working algorithm tested in software simulation; no physical hardware connected.
Unavailable: Transport unsupported on this platform or device.

About xChat

Offline-first, serverless encrypted messenger built in Rust.

Architecture: Rust core workspace (xchat-core, xchat-crypto, xchat-transport, xchat-cli) + native Android WebView & BLE wrappers.

Cryptography: X3DH key agreement, Double Ratchet forward secrecy, ChaCha20-Poly1305 AEAD, Chaumian Sphinx onion routing.

Design System: Holo icon system based on Lucide (MIT License), Nielsen 10 Usability Heuristics, WCAG AA dark palette.

Emergency SOS

Hold the button for 2 seconds to broadcast an encrypted emergency distress beacon to all reachable peers on the local network.
